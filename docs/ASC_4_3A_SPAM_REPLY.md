## Distinct functionality (not a thin template shell)
1. **Live streaming for artists** — “Go Live” with LiveKit (in-app camera) and optional Mux OBS/RTMP for professional broadcasts, plus live viewer rooms and chat.
2. **Ticketed live events** — artists can create free or paid events; fans unlock access with In-App Purchase tickets.
3. **Music UGC platform** — artists upload original tracks with rights attestation; fans discover, follow, like, and play via a global floating player with lock-screen Media Session controls.
4. **Artist tools** — upload, artist dashboard, analytics (plays/revenue), collaboration rooms with timestamped waveform feedback, and Stripe Connect for artist payouts (platform fee model).
5. **Subscriptions via App Store IAP** — Premium / Pro plans through RevenueCat + StoreKit (not a web-only paywall wrapped as an app).
6. **VOD archive** — ended live sessions remain available for replay.
7. **Social layer** — follows, DMs, notifications, and activity feed around artists and streams.
8. **Localization** — multi-language UI for a global independent-artist audience.
9. **Own backend & brand** — Firebase project `kalunez-app`, custom Kalunez branding, legal pages (Terms, Privacy, DMCA), and support at support@kalunez.com.
